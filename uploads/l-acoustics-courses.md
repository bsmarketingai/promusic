- **Slug:** `system-workflow` · **Zdroj:** https://www.l-acoustics.com/our-course/system-workflow/
- **Štítky (learning profiles):** System Technician, System Engineer, Immersive System Engineer, Immersive Mixing Engineer, Audio Professional
- **[THUMB] výpis:** https://www.l-acoustics.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/SystemWorkflow_banner2.jpg
- **[HERO] detail:** https://www.l-acoustics.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/SystemWorkflow_banner2.jpg

**H1:** System & Workflow
**Perex:** Establish your role in the L-Acoustics ecosystem and align your practice with the latest tools and state-of-the-art workflow
**CTA:** Book a session

### Learning objectives
- Understand the L-Acoustics system approach of live sound reproduction
- Know the components of an L-Acoustics system
- Understand the workflow steps of a loudspeaker system project and the associated professional roles
- Know the L-Acoustics tools to use along the project workflow

### Related Products (jen text)
L1 System · LA1.16i · Soundvision Connect · Milan Manager · LA7.16 · LA-RAK III · L2 · L2D · LA7.16i · L-ISA Studio · L-ISA Controller · LA Network Manager · Soundvision · LA-RAK II AVB · KIVA II · KARA II · KARA IIi · K3 · K3i · K2 · K1 · K1-SB · P1 · LA2Xi · LS10 · LA4X · LA12X · L-ISA Processor II Core

### Modalities
| | |
|---|---|
| Prerequisites | Technical English |
| Duration | Onsite: 7 hours<br>Online: 2 virtual classrooms of 2h30min, 1 week apart, with approx. 2h of autonomous work in between |
| Participants | 12 maximum |
| Learning format | Instructor-led session: onsite or online classroom<br>Complementary activities: online education platform |
| Teaching methods | Presentation, demonstration, tutorial |
| Languages | Session delivered in local language<br>Material written in technical English |
| Instructor profile | Live sound expert, selected, trained, certified and regularly updated by L-Acoustics education team |
| Certification | Attendance to the session(s)<br>Completion of online activities |

### Learning Agenda
**ONSITE - Morning or ONLINE - 1/2**
- **SYSTEM APPROACH** (2 hours) – Sound system; L-Acoustics system; Loudspeaker system; Drive system; Rigging system; L-ISA system
- **SMALL-SCALE SYSTEM** (1.5 hour) – Soundvision & LA Network Manager basics; Plug-and-play setup

**ONSITE - Afternoon or ONLINE - 2/2**
- **PROJECT WORKFLOW** (2 hours) – Project; Design; Implementation; Calibration; Operation
- **MEDIUM-SCALE SYSTEM** (1.5 hour) – Theatre system design tutorial; M1 measurement and tuning demonstration

### Required Equipment
| | |
|---|---|
| Instructor-led session | Computer with a mouse and latest version of Soundvision and LA Network Manager software: L-Acoustics website<br>Note pad and pen |
| Online activities | Computer with a mouse and internet connection |

### Earned benefits
Upon completion, the participant is granted with a Certificate and a 1-year account activation/extension on the online L-Acoustics Education platform.
In the platform, the membership to the System & Workflow group opens access to:
- Always up-to-date System & Workflow material in the Learning center
- Forum gathering all the System & Workflow certified learners and instructors

Upon certification, the participant receives 3.5 AVIXA RUs, for renewal of CTS, CTS-D and CTS-I status.

- **Sub-low frequencies in Sound Reinforcement** (2 hours) – Sonic substance; Need for control; Loudspeaker sources
- **Source Directivity Control** (2 hours) – Audience coverage; Rear rejection

**Afternoon**
- **Full System Configuration** (1 hour 30 minutes) – System layouts; Main-sub alignment; Main-fill combination
- **Environmental factors** (1 hour 30 minutes) – Room acoustics; Atmospheric conditions; Audience density
